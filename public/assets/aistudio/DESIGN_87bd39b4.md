## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a tactile, curated digital zine aesthetic tailored for creative portfolios, developer-creators, and editorial personal showcases. It merges the deliberate, physical charm of independent print media with crisp digital utility. 

The visual style lives at the intersection of **Tactile / Skeuomorphic papercraft** and **Editorial Retro-Modernism**:
- **Tone:** Grounded, authentic, playful yet disciplined, artistic, and deeply intentional.
- **Audience:** Creative directors, technical recruiters, startup founders, and collaborative partners seeking genuine creative identity over sterile corporate templates.
- **Sensory Metaphor:** Heavy matte stationery, postal ephemera, hand-cut paper collage layers, vintage olive ink stamping, and archival office accessories (binder clips, washi tape, file tabs).

Every surface evokes physical interaction through layered paper cutouts, scalloped postage stamp contours, polaroid photo mounts, subtle two-tone awning stripes, and playful four-point retro sparkle accents.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an earthy, serene botanical landscape balanced with warm, unbleached fiber paper.

- **Primary (`#8C9A84`)**: Mid-tone sage green serving as the master atmosphere, primary surface background, and signature field tint.
- **Secondary (`#434E3D`)**: Deep forest olive used for high-contrast frames, solid button fills, postage stamp serration borders, and structural outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#B2BEAB`)**: Soft, muted dusty sage for subtle interior dividers, awning stripes, secondary badges, and low-contrast borders.
- **Neutral (`#EFECE6`)**: Warm off-white cardstock cream, offering a soft tactile reading surface for hero editorial titles, major content cards, and tab backgrounds.
- **Named Colors:**
  - `paper-pure` (`#F9F8F5`): Pristine archival paper tone reserved for polaroid photo borders, ticket cards, and elevated callout notes.
  - `ink-charcoal` (`#222620`): Deep, warm forest-tinted charcoal replacing harsh pure black to simulate dense letterpress printing.
  - `ink-soft` (`rgba(34, 38, 32, 0.72)`): Diluted letterpress ink for secondary metadata, captions, and supplementary descriptions.
  - `tape-highlight` (`rgba(239, 236, 230, 0.65)`): Semi-translucent masking tape effect applied across photo corners and cards.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
1. The canvas relies on `primary` (`#8C9A84`) as an immersive backdrop, rather than typical stark white or dark themes.
2. Content containers stack upward in lightness: canvas (`#8C9A84`) &rarr; section backplate (`#EFECE6`) &rarr; focal cards/polaroids (`#F9F8F5`).
3. Structural framing and tactile accents use `secondary` (`#434E3D`) to anchor elements with distinct silhouette definitions.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the imposing, tight-leaded, graphic punch of classic poster advertising with refined modern sans-serif legibility.

- **Display & Headings (`Anton`):** Heavy condensed letterforms set strictly in uppercase with ultra-tight line height (`0.92` to `1.05`). Used for hero title stacks, chapter headers, marquee strips, and pricing amounts. Creates immediate editorial weight.
- **Interface & Content (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, geometric-humanist letterforms designed for comfortable reading on paper-textured card containers. Clean geometric forms ensure dense body copy remains effortlessly legible.
- **Handwritten Accent Tone (`Caveat`):** Used strictly for annotations, scribbled stickers, author notes, arrows, and personal signatures. It breaks formal grid edges with slight counter-clockwise rotations (`-2deg` to `-4deg`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors a curated scrapbook page or architectural editorial zine spread. It relies on a hybrid 12-column grid anchored by modular paper cards that can be pinned, offset, or layered.

### Layout Principles
- **Editorial Grids:** 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`1200px` max content bounds) transitioning to a 6-column grid on tablet, and a single or dual-column stacked layout on mobile.
- **Section Enclosures:** Major sections are often framed as dedicated physical spreads with alternate borders: striped awnings, scalloped top headers, or file tab navigation along top edges.
- **Intentional Overlaps:** Items break baseline constraints—polaroids overlap borders by `1rem` to `1.5rem`, binder clips anchor across top sheet dividers, and washi tape pieces overlap card corners at odd 15-degree angles.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Wide multi-column layout with generous `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Visual ephemera (sparkles, stamps, binder clips) are fully expressed.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Two-to-three column reflow, margins compress to `1.5rem`. Secondary decorations scale to 80%.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Linear stacked cards. Scrapbook items align within safe margins (`1.25rem`). Multi-column card sets convert into vertically stacked scrapbook sheets or horizontally swipeable paper stacks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on sterile digital blur dropshadows or gradient gloss. Instead, elevation communicates **physical paper sheets resting upon a drafting table**.

### Tiers of Tactile Depth
1. **Level 0 (Table Surface):** Flat Sage Green (`#8C9A84`). Often carries a microscopic SVG noise grain (1.5% - 2.5% opacity) or subtle two-tone awning vintage stripes.
2. **Level 1 (Cardstock / Manila Folder):** Card sheets in Warm Cream (`#EFECE6`). Bordered with a solid `1.5px` or `2px` stroke in Deep Forest Olive (`#434E3D`). Shadow: `0 3px 0 rgba(67, 78, 61, 0.18), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(34, 38, 32, 0.12)`.
3. **Level 2 (Photographs & Polaroids):** Pure Cream (`#F9F8F5`) mounts resting on top of cardstock. Shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(34, 38, 32, 0.15), 0 1px 3px rgba(34, 38, 32, 0.08)`. Slight paper tilt (`rotate(-1.5deg)` or `rotate(2deg)`).
4. **Level 3 (Hardware & Overlays):** Binder clips, washi tape ribbons, and 4-point sparkle stars. Masking tape uses subtle color multiply (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`) with crisp directional edges. Metallic binder clips cast crisp downward micro-shadows (`0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`).

### Outlines & Borders
Borders are crisp and intentional. Structural containers leverage dark olive borders (`2px solid #434E3D`). Fine dividers and ticket perforation boundaries utilize dashed or dotted strokes (`1.5px dashed #434E3D` or `dotted #8C9A84`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is predominantly architectural and crisp (`roundedness: 1`), honoring paper cutters, die-cuts, and traditional stamp serrations.

- **Base Radius:** Base radius is `0.25rem` (soft), preventing cards from looking digitally rounded or pill-like, preserving the hand-cut feel.
- **Scalloped Postage Stamp Edges:** Image frames feature decorative radial serrations (`radial-gradient` masks creating half-circle cutout rims) bordered by thick olive outlines.
- **Folder Tabs:** Asymmetrical single-corner roundedness or trapezoidal chamfers on card headers (`border-radius: 8px 8px 0 0` with folder lip accents).
- **Polaroid Frames:** Rectangles with uneven vertical padding (`space-sm` top and sides, `space-xl` bottom chin for handwritten captions).
- **Decorative Accents:** 4-point diamond geometric stars, scalloped badge rims, and angled torn-edge tape bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Forest Olive (`#434E3D`) fill with Warm Cream (`#EFECE6`) bold uppercase text. Border: `2px solid #434E3D`. Radius: `0.375rem`. Shadow: solid tactile drop `0 3px 0 #222620`. On active/pressed: transforms `translateY(3px)` and drops the shadow to zero.
- **Secondary / Outline Action:** Transparent background with `#434E3D` stroke and ink text. Hover fills with pale sage wash (`rgba(140, 154, 132, 0.2)`).
- **Interactive Badge Button:** Small rounded tag with folder-tab styling or dotted outline for secondary filters and navigation.

### Polaroid & Stamp Photo Frames
- **Polaroid Frame:** `#F9F8F5` background, `0.5rem` top/left/right border, `1.75rem` bottom border. Embedded image sits flat inside. Bottom margin accommodates a handwritten caption or category chip.
- **Postage Stamp Frame:** High-contrast frame utilizing CSS mask-image scalloped circles (`mask: radial-gradient(...)`) or SVG scalloped contour border filled with Olive `#434E3D`.

### Content Cards & Folio Sheets
- **Scrapbook Card:** Cardstock body (`#EFECE6`), `2px solid #434E3D` border. Header features an integrated faux folder tab or a top-centered binder clip element.
- **Ticket / Coupon Card:** Divided into two compartments by a vertical or horizontal dashed perforation line (`border-left: 2px dashed #434E3D`) with semi-circular notch cutouts at the ends.

### Chips & Tags
- Compact editorial labels in all-caps `Anton` or bold `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Outlined with `1.5px solid #434E3D`, filled with `#F9F8F5` or `#B2BEAB`.
- Micro tape clips placed at tag edges for scrapbook attachment aesthetic.

### Input Fields
- Heavy cream background (`#F9F8F5`) surrounded by a distinct `1.5px solid #434E3D` outline.
- Placeholder text in soft ink (`rgba(34, 38, 32, 0.4)`).
- On focus: Outline thickens to `2px solid #434E3D` with an offset highlight ring in sage (`0 0 0 3px #B2BEAB`). No rounded pill styles; maintains crisp `0.25rem` corners.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square (`checkbox`) and diamond or circle (`radio`) indicators outlined with `2px solid #434E3D`.
- Check state: Filled with `#434E3D` featuring a crisp off-white (`#EFECE6`) hand-drawn style checkmark or inner solid square.

### Ephemera & Accents
- **Binder Clips:** Centered top-mounted SVG/CSS hardware spanning card edges with metallic charcoal and brass tones.
- **Masking Tape:** Angled rectangular strips (`height: ~18px`, `width: ~60px`) in translucent cream (`rgba(239, 236, 230, 0.7)`), overlapping card corners.
- **Four-Point Sparkles:** Placed strategically near headers, polaroids, and floating badges to provide editorial rhythm and visual interest.
